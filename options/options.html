Settings

Movie sites frequently change their domains (e.g. .dad to .top). Use this page to grant permissions for new domains without waiting for an extension update.

1. Movie Source Domains

Add domains where you browse movies (e.g. *://*.new-desire.com/*). This allows the extension to intercept download clicks.

    2. Bypass Chain Domains

    Add domains used in the redirect chain (e.g. *://*.new-gdflix.com/*). This allows the extension to auto-click buttons and solve Captchas.
